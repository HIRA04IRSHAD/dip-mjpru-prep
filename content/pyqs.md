# All past papers

Every MJPRU Digital Image Processing paper we could collect, typed out question by question. The tag after each question says which unit of the **CS-403T** syllabus it belongs to, and **(old)** marks topics that were in the older EC-458T syllabus but are **not** in CS-403T (compression, wavelets, transforms).

::: tip Where these came from
2025-26 end-sem and both 2025 mids: photographs of the actual papers from our batch. 2017, 2018, 2019: MJPRU B.Tech (EC branch, EC-458T) Digital Image Processing papers from mjpruonline.com. Same university, same textbook (Gonzalez & Woods), and the same examiners' habits, so they are very useful for predicting questions. MJPRU DIP papers for 2020–2024 are not available online; if you have one, add it.
:::

**Paper pattern (end-sem):** 3 hours, 70 marks, 8 questions, attempt **any five**, each 14 marks, usually split (a) 7 + (b) 7.
**Paper pattern (mid-sem):** 1 hour, 10 marks, 4–5 short questions, attempt all.
