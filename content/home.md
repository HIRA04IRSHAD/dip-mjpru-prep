## The syllabus (CS-403T)

| Unit | Topics |
|---|---|
| **I** | Introduction and fundamentals; components of a DIP system; visual perception; image model; sampling and quantization. Spatial enhancement: grey-level transforms, piecewise-linear (contrast stretching), histogram equalization and specification, local enhancement, arithmetic/logic operations, spatial filtering, mean and order-statistic filters, Laplacian |
| **II** | Frequency domain: Fourier transform, filtering basics, low-pass, high-pass, Gaussian LPF/HPF, homomorphic filtering. Restoration: degradation model, noise models, mean and order-statistic filters, periodic noise reduction (band filters), minimum mean-square-error (Wiener) restoration |
| **III** | Colour: fundamentals, colour models, conversions, colour transformations, smoothing and sharpening, colour segmentation. Morphology: logic operations, dilation, erosion, opening, closing, boundary extraction, region filling, connected components, convex hull, thinning, thickening |
| **IV** | Registration: geometric transformation, mapping, stereo imaging, correspondence, recovering depth. Segmentation: region extraction, pixel-based, multilevel and local thresholding, region-based, edge and line detection, edge operators, edge linking, corner detection |
| **V** | Feature extraction: representation, topological and geometric attributes, boundary and region description. Object recognition: deterministic methods, clustering, statistical classification, syntactic recognition, tree search, graph matching |

**Books:** R.C. Gonzalez & R.E. Woods, *Digital Image Processing*, 3rd ed.; Gonzalez, Woods & Eddins, *DIP using MATLAB*.
